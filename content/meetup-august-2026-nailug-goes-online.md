Summary: From physical meetups to our very first online meetup!

We had our 7th meet-up of the year on the 1st of August 2026.
As compared to the previous meet-ups, this one happened
online. Yes! you read that right. We had it online.

# Why?

That is a valid question dear reader. In the [last
blog post](https://nairobilug.org/2026/07/meetup-July-2026.html) ,
we had promised to meet at Mount Kenya University Nairobi Campus.
This could not happen because the spaces were being prepared for
students to take their final semester exams. We
got this piece of information late. We tried to get alternative
meeting spaces but they were all booked or unavailbe. With 16 hours to the
meet-up, we pivoted and decided to have it online.

# How did it go?

It was a success. Twenty people showed up. We sent details of the
change in less than 24 hours to the time the meet-up was to start.
The star of the show was [Yusuf Hersi](https://www.hersitech.com/) .
He did a demo of how [NextDeploy](https://nextdeploy.org/) could be
used to deploy [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/)
websites onto a linux server. He also explained the design decisions
he made during the development process. The audience was very impressed
with the project itself, the effort that went into it and the final
product and documentation. Great work Yusuf!

He is looking for feedback on NextDeploy. Any thoughts, comments, opinions or
sentiments on the project will be highly appreciated. Share it with any Next.js
users you know so that the project can get the traction it deserves.

NextDeploy is a tool made to empower Next.js developers to deploy their Next.js
websites on Linux servers, AWS and Cloudflare without relying on
[Vercel tooling or infrastructure](https://vercel.com/docs/frameworks/full-stack/nextjs).
Check out the [NextDeploy's Github Repository](https://github.com/aynaash/NextDeploy)
, star it, fork it and contribute to it.

To better understand the project, read the [NextDeploy documentation](https://nextdeploy.org/docs).
It explains how the project can be installed, the commands one needs to run
and a technical reference section which highlights ways one can secure their Next.js
deployment when using NextDeploy.
